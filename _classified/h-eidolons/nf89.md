To survive, NF became a hitman. Each assignment nearly killed NF. NF would replace each injury with a metal equivalent, slowly transforming into an unstoppable mech-icon hybrid. Soon enough, NF ran the entire Northern H-city mafia, influencing the political climate.  <a href="/classified-files/h-eidolons/hhyper/">HHyper</a> saw his potential as an unstoppable juggernaut and hired him to be an Eidolon. NF worked closely with Fellow Eidolon members, namely <a href="/classified-files/h-eidolons/broskm/">Broskm</a>, who further empowered his mechanical implants.
