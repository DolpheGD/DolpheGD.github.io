<tr><th markdown="span">Eidolon Researcher specializes in biotech mech creation and Void research. After successfully inventing the prototype of "Voidwarp," Broskm was sucked into the Voidland Ravine that was linked to the H-City research facility with experimental Voidwarp tech. Broskm was captured by the Eidolon and brought to Hhyper. Hhyper recognized his potential, and HHyper hijacked Broskm’s brain and forced him to do HHyper’s bidding. Broskm was quickly appointed as the top researcher of the Eidolons. It is known that he committed cruel and twisted experiments at the Void Crevasse Research Facility. Broskm is Hhypers' top researcher in Void applications.  HHyper saw the extreme potential for Broskm to improve NF’s combat capabilities. While Broskm was never a fighter, he heavily experimented with Mech-Flesh hybrids. Reports indicate he has a high tolerance to the negative effects of the void, similar to Stubby.
