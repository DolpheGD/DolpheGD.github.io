<tr><th markdown="span">Dolpo was a tier 3 gunner for an Aerion Mk1 airship. During the events of <a href="/classified-files/h-machines/project-novaform/">Project Novaform</a>, Dolpo would crash after his Aerion ship was split in half. He is presumed dead. Dolpo’s origin stems from being an ordinary <a href="/classified-files/acatrya/">Acatrya</a> Citizen, raised alongside his brother Dolphin. Dolpo grew increasingly paranoid of the Cascade insurgency ever since <a href="/classified-files/geographic-locations/glacier-15/">Glacier 15</a>\. He began to express radical views toward Acatrya’s people, to his brother's disgust. His mental state kept deteriorating, and his brother eventually severed ties with Dolpo. After realizing Cascade’s leader, <a href="/classified-files/captains/dolphe/">Dolphe</a>, had a similar name to his, he snapped. Dolpo defected to H-nation and vowed to destroy all of his kind. While clumsy and weak, Dolpo quickly rose through the H-ranks through sheer determination and hatred for Acatrya and Cascade. Dolpo would train his aim every day to never miss a single shot. Reports said he would train nonstop to the point of mania. *Refer to the Boss/Enemy Files for more information.*</th><th markdown="span">![image97](/assets/classified-files/images/image97.png) ![image98](/assets/classified-files/images/image98.png)</th></tr>
