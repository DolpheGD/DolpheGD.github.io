<tr><th markdown="span">The Ocellios Defense system is located in the heart of the Ocellios Lab security sector, adjacent to the underground lab and bio sectors. The system is comprised of an autonomous monitor that is linked to all security systems of the entire defense complex. This includes SAJ, SAJ II, and the Eruptor Trio.   The security system automatically detects intruders and allocates resources to eliminate them. Similar to SAJ, it controls turrets, cameras, and mechanical blades across the facility. If the Primary monitor is destroyed, authority of command goes down to the next lowest, where they are still able to function and coordinate together. As such, the Ocellios Defense System can potentially use any weapon or attack the system has access to in the lab, making it a critical threat and unwise to engage. Defeating it is pointless, as weapons will continue to function without the core system.
