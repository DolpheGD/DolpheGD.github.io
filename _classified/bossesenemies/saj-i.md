<tr><th markdown="span">SAJ I is a tertiary defense mechanism controlled by the Ocellios Defense Systems. Its only role is eliminating intruders. It is equipped with multiple energy blasters, electric arc generators, missiles, and lasers. Boosters in the back help SAJ accelerate and decelerate quickly. SAJ exhibits a unique ability to split, with its after-image being an alternate version of SAJ with a blade. It is, however, weak to electricity-based powers, as its cooling system can easily be overloaded due to its high energy consumption. SAJ is also connected to the other defense systems of the defense sector. This includes turrets, sawblades, cameras, and mechanical arms.  Multiple logs show that SAJ was never supposed to be stationed outside, and doing so may cause mass destruction to innocent civilians.
