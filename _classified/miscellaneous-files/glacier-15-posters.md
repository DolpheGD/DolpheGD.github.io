JFC was an upcoming low-budget movie directed by local actors in the city of Glacier 15, one of whom was Rex. The film was never released, and no copy of it exists. It is considered lost media, and sometimes <a href="/classified-files/world-aligners/josh/">Josh</a> travels to Glacier 15 to attempt to find this film. The next poster recovered was a bounty put up for a carrot. This carrot refers to an alias of a <a href="/classified-files/team-cascade/">Team Cascade</a> member, who currently works in a tertiary Cascade base in Stormpoint City. The last poster is an outed resistance movement that formed locally in Glacier 15\. This movement was separate from Team Cascade.
