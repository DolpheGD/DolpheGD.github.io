<tr><th markdown="span">The Crawfish is an extremely rare cascade ship model designed for maneuverability and Voidwarp. Unlike other models, this ship can carry two passengers inside. The Crawfish model used to be popular from 105-107 IC. However, this model was phased out due to their more fragile design. The surviving Crawfish models only exist because they survived the test of time, Voidwarp after Voidwarp. This model was also phased out because it did not serve any special purpose to warrant the cost of manufacturing. Other ship models served more specific purposes better, while The Crawfish was only “okay” at multiple things.  Can fly at low-mid altitudes.
