<tr><th markdown="span">The mechanism is an experimental ship design engineered by <a href="/classified-files/captains/virtual/">Virtual</a>, <a href="/classified-files/aero-squad/andy/">Andy</a>, <a href="/classified-files/aero-squad/fax/">FAX</a>, BLANKK, Gai, BLK, BLAK, <a href="/classified-files/captains/caliper/">Caliper</a>, and BLANKKKK. Its sole purpose is to magnetically transport portals to other locations. It can be remotely and automatically operated, but interestingly has enough space in the cabin for two. The reasoning for this is unknown and likely stems from the poor planning rather than intentional design. The engineering quality of the Mechanism is respectable; however, it has not been tested in real combat until the <a href="/classified-files/h-machines/project-novaform/">Project Novaform</a> incident. The future use of the Mechanism is yet to be explored, although it is likely that this will remain the only model of it. No license is required to ride in it, although a special license is needed to remotely operate one.</th><th markdown="span">![image69](/assets/classified-files/images/image69.png)</th></tr>
