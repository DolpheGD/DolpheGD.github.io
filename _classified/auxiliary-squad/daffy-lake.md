<tr><th markdown="span">Daffy and Lake are two siblings who joined Team Cascade after their parents were killed in Xender’s Void Instability project. The event was covered up, and Daffy and Lake were silenced from ever speaking.  They exhibit average physical strength with no special abilities. During the Ocellios Mission, Daffy and Lake hijacked a train heading towards the Eidolon Stronghold. They grabbed experimental weapons from Stubby’s Lab while Dolphe infiltrated and drew the attention of major defense systems. This superweapon was a key component in defeating the Eidolons in the Mission. However, a missile struck the train directly, causing their death.</th><th markdown="span">![image42](/assets/classified-files/images/image42.png) ![image43](/assets/classified-files/images/image43.png)</th></tr>
