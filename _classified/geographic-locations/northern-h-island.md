<tr><th markdown="span">Northern H-Island is a separate territory in <a href="/classified-files/h-nation/">H-Nation</a>, which operates under its own sub-leadership. It has a cold climate with little variation. The weather is frequently cloudy, with some rainstorms and hurricanes. The terrain is flat around the edges, with rocky mountains near the center. Northern H-Island is the center for crime and illegal business in H-Nation. Little regulation exists, and <a href="/classified-files/h-eidolons/hhyper/">HHyper</a> does not choose to enforce stricter regulations. Gangs and territorial disputes are common here.  Additionally, Northern H-Island invented a series of arenas where citizens can battle each other to earn credits. People can also bet with credits. This serves as entertainment for many civilians.</th><th markdown="span">![image164](/assets/classified-files/images/image164.png)</th></tr>
