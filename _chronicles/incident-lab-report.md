## INCIDENT REPORT

### Case file \#1025J05H

#### May 23rd 109IC Documentation sent to HHHQ

04:15  
\> Security systems reported an explosion in cell block B, and fire suppression systems were activated. Cell block B initiated a full flood of Ozone into the air to instantly kill all occupants.

04:23  
\> Security personnel were dispatched to cell block B which at 04:25 sent immediate distress and set <a href="/classified-files/miscellaneous-files/void/">Void</a> Crevasse Research Facility into lockdown. Reports from security logs note damage in corridors \-2N, \-2E, Lift A, 4E, leading to Terminal System B.

04:28  
\> System administration reported that a 4.87 MB file was copied; the contents of this file contained Ocellios Extraction plans, <a href="/classified-files/stubby/">Stubby</a> project files, and superweapon blueprints whose contents should be encrypted.

04:32  
\> Eidolon <a href="/classified-files/h-eidolons/broskm/">Broskm</a> confronted this threat, dubbed “<a href="/classified-files/other-units/subject-29/">Subject 29</a>” and proceeded to attempt to neutralize it. It fled further into the facility in an attempt to escape out the top helipad. Along the way, key experimental void tech was stolen and used on staff and security personnel. The threat proceeded to steal an aircraft and attempt an escape.

04:38  
\>  Activation of SAM missiles and T.A.L.O.N aircraft authorized by Broskm. Neutralization of Subject 29 was successful at 04:45 at 54,000 ft, and the remains fell deep into the Void crevasse.
